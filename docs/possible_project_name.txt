## Revisit Note — Why I Called the Project *Icarus*

I chose **Icarus** because the name works on different levels.

- A drone flies.
- I went into the project with very ambitious expectations about building almost everything from scratch.
- As the project developed, I learned that real robotics work often means understanding, integrating, debugging, and validating existing systems rather than reinventing every component.
- The project also represented a desire to reach beyond what I already knew and push into unfamiliar areas like ROS 2, PX4, SLAM, LiDAR odometry, and autonomy.

So *Icarus* is not just a reference to flight or failure. For me, it represents **ambition, experimentation, confronting limits, and learning through reaching beyond them**.

The name is deliberately **symbolically layered** rather than having one fixed meaning.
